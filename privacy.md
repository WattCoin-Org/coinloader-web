# Privacy & Refund Policy

This page explains what data CoinLoader.com handles and how refunds work. CoinLoader.com is operated by AIguy.

## Data handling

We don't share your data with third parties. Our bots run locally, inside your own Grok Bot container or your own terminal, and read only public sources such as blockchain RPC nodes and public market data endpoints from your environment. No wallet keys, seed phrases or files are uploaded to us. The only requests that reach our license server are the license steps: requesting a quote, purchasing, claiming a `wcl_...` key and checking that a SKU is licensed; these carry the license key and SKU. Runtime downloads are served per license with a watermark block.

## Payments

CoinLoader.com accepts SOL only. Payments are made from your own wallet and verified on-chain from the public transaction; we never hold your funds or keys.

## Refund policy

SOL purchases are final. The one exception: if you paid but did not receive a working license and we can't fix it, we refund the same SOL amount to the wallet that paid. Any license issued for a refunded order is revoked, and network fees are not refunded. To ask about a refund, use the [support email on the contact page](https://coinloader.com/contact.html) and include your SKU and order ID or transaction signature.

## Not advice

CoinLoader products report factual on-chain and static-analysis findings. They are not financial, legal or tax advice.
